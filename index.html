Chiropractic services in Hornsby

Neck pain

Lower back pain

Headaches

About

Dr Andy H.G. Lee, Chiropractor

Dr Andy H.G. Lee (Hyo Geun Lee) graduated in Chiropractic Science from Macquarie University, holds General registration as a chiropractor and practises at Hornsby Chiropractor, our Hornsby chiropractic clinic.
Care starts with your symptoms, relevant history and movement, followed by an explanation of the findings.
Andy discusses suitable treatment options, expected benefits and risks, alternatives, and referral where appropriate.
The clinic provides care for neck and lower back concerns, headaches accompanied by neck or shoulder symptoms, and shoulder pain or restricted movement, including concerns about frozen shoulder.
Treatment suitability and outcomes vary between individuals.
