Headaches

Headache and neck pain care in Hornsby

Some headaches arrive with a stiff neck, tight shoulders or pain that starts at the base of the skull. Tension-type headaches often feel like a band of pressure around the head and can come with tenderness in the neck and shoulder muscles (Mayo Clinic).

At our Hornsby clinic we look at the pattern of your headaches and the way your neck moves, discuss whether chiropractic care is suitable, and recommend medical review when a headache needs it.

Who we see

Headaches people commonly book for

  • Headaches with neck stiffness or shoulder tension
  • Pain starting at the base of the skull
  • Headaches that build during desk work, screen use or long drives
  • Headaches linked with jaw clenching or teeth grinding, alongside dental advice
  • Headaches after exercise, once a medical cause has been considered

Headaches have many causes, and not all of them involve the neck. Migraine, medication-related headaches and other medical conditions need different care. Using pain medicines frequently can itself lead to ongoing headaches (Mayo Clinic).

Assessment

What happens at your appointment

We ask when the headaches started, how often they occur, what they feel like, what triggers or eases them, and which medicines you use. The examination includes neck and upper back movement and muscle tenderness, with a basic neurological screen where appropriate.

A headache diary kept for a couple of weeks before your visit can be useful. Note the time, possible triggers, activities, sleep and any medicines taken.

Care options

Care options we may discuss

When headaches appear related to the neck, options may include hands-on treatment of the neck and upper back, exercises, and changes to your workstation, screen use or sleep set-up. Responses vary between people, and we will tell you if medical review would be more appropriate than chiropractic care.

Before treatment we discuss the expected benefits, risks, alternatives and fees. Neck treatment has specific risks, which we explain in relation to your health history.

Before booking

When a headache needs urgent care

Call 000 or go to an emergency department for:

  • A sudden, severe headache that peaks within seconds or minutes
  • Headache with fever and a stiff neck, or with confusion
  • Headache with weakness, numbness, slurred speech, vision changes or loss of balance
  • Headache after a head injury

A new or changing headache pattern, particularly if you are over 50, should be checked promptly by your GP (healthdirect).

Questions

Common questions about headache care

Do I need to see a doctor first?

Not for a private appointment. However, a new, severe, worsening or unusual headache should be assessed by a doctor before chiropractic care is considered.

Can neck problems cause headaches?

Headache can be referred from structures in the neck, which is sometimes called cervicogenic headache. Tension-type headaches can also come with tight, tender neck and shoulder muscles. An assessment helps work out which pattern fits.

Should I keep taking pain medicine?

Discuss pain medicines with your GP or pharmacist. If you need them on many days each month, talk to your GP, because frequent use can contribute to ongoing headaches (Mayo Clinic).
