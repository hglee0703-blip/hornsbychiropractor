Services

Hornsby chiropractic care

Chiropractic care at Hornsby Chiropractor - Andy Lee starts with understanding your symptoms, movement and daily needs. After an individual assessment, we explain the findings and discuss suitable treatment options or referral. Our clinic is at Suite 4, 43A Florence Street, Hornsby NSW 2077.

Neck pain

Care for neck pain and stiffness

Care for neck pain and stiffness starts with understanding your symptoms, relevant history and movement. We discuss suitable chiropractic treatment options, their expected benefits and risks, alternatives, and referral where appropriate.

Lower back pain

Care for lower back pain

We consider your lower back symptoms, movement and daily activities, then discuss suitable treatment options and an individual care plan or referral where appropriate. Symptoms alone do not establish a diagnosis.

Headaches

Headache and neck pain care

For headaches accompanied by neck or shoulder symptoms, we assess the pattern of symptoms and discuss whether chiropractic care or medical review is appropriate. Suitable treatment options depend on the assessment. A sudden, severe or unusual headache needs prompt medical assessment.

Shoulder pain

Shoulder pain and movement care

A consultation considers shoulder pain, stiffness and restricted movement, including concerns about frozen shoulder. We discuss the findings, suitable treatment options and referral if needed; different shoulder problems can need different care.

Chiropractic consultation and care

Your consultation includes discussion of your symptoms and relevant history, movement assessment and an explanation of the findings. We discuss suitable treatment options, including expected benefits, risks, alternatives and whether referral is appropriate.

Decisions about care are made with you. Treatment suitability and outcomes vary between individuals.
