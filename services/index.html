Disc pain and chiropractic care in Hornsby

Assessment and conservative treatment of disc-related neck and lower back pain are a central focus at Hornsby Chiropractor. We care for people with severe pain and symptoms that travel into an arm or leg, where chiropractic care is appropriate after an individual assessment. Our clinic is at Suite 4, 43A Florence Street, Hornsby NSW 2077.

Our central focus

Care for disc-related pain

Disc-related neck or lower back pain can make standing, walking or even small movements extremely difficult. Pain may also radiate into an arm or leg. We assess your symptoms, movement and nerve function to discuss whether conservative treatment is suitable or medical referral is needed.

Dr Andy Lee (chiropractor) has cared for patients whose disc-related pain left them unable to stand or move, including people with radiating arm or leg pain. His clinical experience includes patients whose pain and movement improved during care after a surgeon had recommended disc surgery.

These individual experiences do not establish that chiropractic care caused the improvement or predict another person's outcome. They are not a promise of disc healing, recovery or avoiding surgery.

Where appropriate, we offer individualised conservative treatment with the aim of managing pain and supporting everyday movement. If surgery has been recommended, we discuss whether this care has a role alongside your specialist's advice. Treatment must not delay necessary surgery or urgent medical care.

Before booking

When severe symptoms need urgent care

If pain leaves you unable to stand or move, seek prompt medical assessment. New or worsening limb weakness or numbness around the groin needs urgent medical assessment.

Call 000 if back or neck pain comes with loss of feeling or movement in a limb, or new problems controlling your bladder or bowels. See Healthdirect's spinal disc guidance.

Neck pain

Care for neck pain and stiffness

Care for neck pain and stiffness starts with understanding your symptoms, relevant history and movement. We discuss suitable chiropractic treatment options, their expected benefits and risks, alternatives, and referral where appropriate.

Lower back pain

Care for lower back pain

We consider your lower back symptoms, movement and daily activities, then discuss suitable treatment options and an individual care plan or referral where appropriate. Symptoms alone do not establish a diagnosis.

Headaches

Headache and neck pain care

For headaches accompanied by neck or shoulder symptoms, we assess the pattern of symptoms and discuss whether chiropractic care or medical review is appropriate. Suitable treatment options depend on the assessment. A sudden, severe or unusual headache needs prompt medical assessment.

Read more about headache care

Shoulder pain

Shoulder pain and movement care

A consultation considers shoulder pain, stiffness and restricted movement, including concerns about frozen shoulder. We discuss the findings, suitable treatment options and referral if needed; different shoulder problems can need different care.

Read more about frozen shoulder care

Chiropractic consultation and care

Your consultation includes discussion of your symptoms and relevant history, movement assessment and an explanation of the findings. We discuss suitable treatment options, including expected benefits, risks, alternatives and whether referral is appropriate.

Decisions about care are made with you. Treatment suitability and outcomes vary between individuals.
