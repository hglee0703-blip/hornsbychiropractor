Shoulder Pain When Sleeping: Why It Happens

26 October 2026 · Andy Lee, Chiropractor · Shoulder pain

You settle onto your usual side, then a dull ache builds in the shoulder underneath you. Rolling over helps briefly, but the discomfort returns or wakes you later. Shoulder pain when sleeping commonly feels worse because body weight presses on an already sensitive area, or because the arm stays in one position for a long time.

The position may aggravate the pain without being its original cause. Problems involving the rotator cuff, bursa, shoulder joint or neck can all be more noticeable at night, and they are not always easy to distinguish from symptoms alone (Healthdirect Australia).

Why shoulder pain when sleeping can feel worse

Lying directly on a sore shoulder adds pressure around the joint and may compress irritated tendons or the bursa. Rotator cuff problems can produce an ache at night, particularly when lying on the affected side (Mayo Clinic).

Your arm position matters as well. Sleeping with the arm overhead, tucked under a pillow or pulled across the body can hold the shoulder near the edge of its comfortable range. A pillow that leaves your head tilted may also contribute to neck discomfort, which can sometimes be felt around the shoulder or arm (Healthdirect Australia).

Common sources of night-time shoulder pain

Rotator cuff irritation is one possibility. The rotator cuff is a group of muscles and tendons that helps move and stabilise the shoulder. Pain may be felt over the outer upper arm and can become more obvious with lifting, reaching or lying on that side (Mayo Clinic). If reaching is also difficult, this guide to shoulder pain when reaching overhead covers some useful distinctions.

Bursitis involves irritation of a small fluid-filled sac that reduces friction near a joint. Shoulder bursitis can cause tenderness and pain with movement or pressure on the area (Mayo Clinic). It often overlaps with tendon problems, so the painful spot alone does not confirm which structure is involved.

Frozen shoulder usually causes increasing pain and marked stiffness, followed by a prolonged period of restricted movement. Night pain can occur during its painful stage (Mayo Clinic). Arthritis can also make the joint sore and stiff, while pain from an irritated nerve in the neck may travel towards the shoulder, arm or hand (Healthdirect Australia; Mayo Clinic).

Clues that help narrow down the cause

Notice what happens during the day. Pain with lifting or lowering the arm may point towards a shoulder tendon problem, while substantial loss of movement in several directions raises the possibility of a stiff joint. Tingling, numbness or pain extending below the elbow makes the neck and nerves more relevant (Healthdirect Australia; Mayo Clinic).

Also check whether turning or tilting your neck changes the symptoms. If you wake with both neck and shoulder discomfort, the suggestions in relieving neck pain after sleeping may be useful. These clues guide an examination, but they cannot reliably diagnose the problem on their own.

What to try in bed tonight

Start by avoiding direct pressure on the painful side for a few nights. On your other side, hug a pillow or place one beneath the sore forearm so the arm is supported rather than hanging across your chest. If you sleep on your back, a folded towel or low pillow beneath the elbow may keep the shoulder in a more relaxed position.

Check your head pillow too. It should fill the gap between your head and mattress without pushing your neck sharply sideways or forwards. There is no single pillow height that suits everyone, so make a small change and judge it by comfort rather than by a product label.

During the day, keep the shoulder moving within a tolerable range unless a clinician has advised otherwise. Do not force stretches through sharp pain or repeatedly test a painful movement. Healthdirect recommends seeking professional advice if shoulder pain is severe, follows an injury, or does not improve with self-care (Healthdirect Australia). A pharmacist or GP can advise whether pain medicine is appropriate for you.

When the shoulder should be assessed

Arrange an assessment if the pain keeps disturbing sleep, movement is becoming restricted, the arm feels weak, or symptoms are not settling. An examination usually considers the shoulder, neck and upper back rather than assuming the painful spot is the source. Seek prompt medical care after a significant fall or injury, or if the shoulder looks deformed, is very swollen, or you cannot use the arm. Sudden shoulder or arm discomfort with chest pressure, sweating, nausea or shortness of breath can be a heart attack symptom and warrants calling Triple Zero (000) (Healthdirect Australia).

Frequently asked questions

These are common practical questions about sore shoulders at night.

Should I stop sleeping on the painful shoulder?

Temporarily avoiding that side is sensible if direct pressure clearly aggravates it. Try the opposite side with the painful arm supported on a pillow, or sleep on your back with support beneath the elbow. Position changes may reduce irritation, but persistent pain still deserves assessment.

Can a pillow cause shoulder pain?

A pillow may contribute if it leaves your neck markedly tilted or allows the upper arm to hang without support. Neck problems can refer discomfort towards the shoulder and arm (Healthdirect Australia). Change one part of the setup at a time so you can tell whether it helps.

Why does my shoulder hurt at night but not much during the day?

Pressure on the shoulder and holding one position can expose sensitivity that is less obvious while you are moving. Rotator cuff pain is often troublesome at night and when lying on the affected side (Mayo Clinic). Night pain is not specific to one diagnosis, particularly if stiffness or neck symptoms are also present.

How long should I wait before seeking help?

Seek help sooner if pain is severe, follows an injury, is accompanied by weakness or numbness, or is steadily worsening. Otherwise, arrange an assessment if simple position changes and sensible activity modification are not helping, or if broken sleep continues (Healthdirect Australia).
