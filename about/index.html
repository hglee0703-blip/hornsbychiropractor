About

Dr Andy H.G. Lee, chiropractor

Dr Andy H.G. Lee (Hyo Geun Lee) is the chiropractor at Hornsby Chiropractor, Suite 4, 43A Florence Street, Hornsby. He graduated in Chiropractic Science from Macquarie University in Sydney and holds General registration as a chiropractor with the Chiropractic Board of Australia through Ahpra. Chiropractors are not medical doctors.

Approach to care

Assessment first, then options discussed with you

Care starts with your symptoms, relevant history and movement, followed by an explanation of the findings. Andy discusses suitable treatment options, their expected benefits and risks, alternatives, fees and referral where appropriate. Decisions about care are made with you, and you can ask questions or decline any part of treatment.

Treatment suitability and outcomes vary between individuals.

Clinical focus

Disc-related pain and common spinal concerns

Assessment and conservative care of disc-related neck and lower back pain, including severe pain and symptoms that travel into an arm or leg, is a central focus of the clinic. Andy also sees people with neck pain, lower back pain, headaches with neck or shoulder symptoms, and shoulder pain or stiffness, including frozen shoulder.
